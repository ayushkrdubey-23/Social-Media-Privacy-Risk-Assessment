Privacy by design

Built for awareness, not surveillance.

01

Self-reported

Assessments use voluntary questionnaire responses rather than account inspection.

02

Synthetic data

Development analytics use fictional synthetic assessment records.

03

Minimal storage

Derived scores, findings, and timestamps are stored without raw questionnaire responses.

04

Defensive scope

The framework does not scrape, track, enumerate, or access private accounts.

Educational disclaimer

Risk scores, weights, and thresholds are educational assumptions. They should not be treated as professional security guarantees or as definitive predictions of privacy harm.
